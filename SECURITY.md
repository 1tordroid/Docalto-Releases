# Security and privacy

## Privacy

Docalto processes documents on your phone. It does not upload, share or collect your documents, and there is no account. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, which may collect device data; Premium has no ads. The full details, including crash reports, Google Play services and on-device AI packs, are in the privacy policy:

- Privacy policy: https://docalto.tordroid.com/privacy/
- Terms: https://docalto.tordroid.com/terms/
- Data safety summary: https://docalto.tordroid.com/#offline

## Get the app safely

Docalto is distributed **only through Google Play**: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tordroid.docalto

This repository has no APK or app files, and Tordroid does not publish Docalto APKs anywhere else. A copy from any other source is not ours. The app checks that it was installed from Google Play and warns you if the copy was modified.

## Reporting a security problem

Please report security issues privately, not in a public issue:

- Telegram: https://t.me/Tordroid_admin

Include the app version (Settings > What's new), your phone model and Android version, and steps to reproduce.
