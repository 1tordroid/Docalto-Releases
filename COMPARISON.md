## What Docalto 2.6.0 actually does

Docalto is an **Android-only** PDF and document app (Android 8 and later). The All Tools list in 2.6.0 contains **75 tools**. Reading, editing, merging, splitting, compressing, converting, scanning, OCR, translation, flows and the smart tools run on the phone. The app does not upload documents, does not require an account, and does not add a watermark to files. Online features (including optional AI that uses a key you supply) stay off until you turn them on. Keys stay encrypted on the phone.

All files access is optional and is asked for only in My Files; every tool still works through Android's file picker. On phones with Google Play services, scanning and QR use Google's scanners, so Docalto does not open the camera. On phones without those services, an in-app scanner asks for the camera first.

Free users may see Google AdMob ads, including a rewarded ad that can unlock some tools. Premium (monthly, yearly, lifetime, or a promo pass) removes ads and the daily limit. Documents are never sent to ads or crash reports.

Docalto is not a Windows, macOS, iOS or web editor, and it is not a full Word/Excel/PowerPoint suite in the way WPS Office or Microsoft 365 is.

## The honest headline

Many merge, split, compress, edit, scan and sign tools already exist in other apps. Several **desktop** products already run a large toolkit **offline** on a computer. Several **cloud** sites already run a large toolkit after you upload a file.

What is uncommon is putting a large toolkit — including split by size, pages on one sheet or booklet order, a PDF/A archive copy, compare, repair, File Rescue, certificate signatures, and a multi-step Flow — on an **Android phone**, with **processing on the device**, **no account**, and **no document upload**, plus the option to bring your own AI key. That is the comparison Docalto can honestly make. It is not true that no other product offers these tools; they are common on PCs and on cloud websites. They are uncommon as an on-device phone kit.

## How to read the table

- **Yes** means official pages describe the capability.
- **Paid** means it is listed behind a subscription or licence.
- **Cloud** means official pages say the file is uploaded or processed on a server, or is saved to that company's storage by default.
- **Mixed** means official pages say it depends on the tool, or both a local and a cloud path exist.
- **Not found** means it was not described on the official pages read for this pass — not a proof that the function is impossible in that product.
- Microsoft Lens is included only as context: new scans ended on 9 March 2026. The current Microsoft path is the OneDrive app scanner (and, in some Microsoft 365 apps, a scan-to-PDF action). Those paths require a Microsoft account and, for OneDrive, do not save the scan only on the device.

## Only in Docalto

**Read this first.** "Only in Docalto" means: among the leading Android document apps compared in this document, in November 2026, we did not find the same combination or the same form on the official pages we read. It is **not** a claim that no other product on earth has a related function. Smaller or newer apps, desktop software and self-hosted tools are outside this list; some of them already offer pieces of this (for example, a few smaller PDF apps also accept a customer-supplied Gemini or Claude key). Desktop products such as Adobe Acrobat Pro, PDF24 Creator, Sejda Desktop, iLovePDF Desktop and Stirling PDF already cover many toolkit jobs on a computer.

Among the leading Android apps in the table, we **did** verify these points as distinctive:

1. **A large on-device toolkit with no account and no document upload.** 75 tools, including Flow, split by size, booklet or n-up pages, PDF/A, compare, repair, certificate signatures and File Rescue, running on the phone. Individual tools exist in other products, especially on PCs and cloud sites. The on-device phone combination is what we did not find in the leading apps above.
2. **Your own AI key, for Gemini, Claude, OpenAI or xAI, stored encrypted on the phone, with Online mode off until you allow it.** The leading apps above either have no generative AI, or offer the company's own assistant (Adobe AI Assistant, WPS AI, iLovePDF AI) rather than a customer-supplied key for those providers.
3. **File Rescue, plus a wide image-format list** that includes RAW previews, Photoshop, JPEG XL, TIFF and a range of older or professional formats. Not found on the official pages of the compared mobile and cloud apps.
4. **Fill a cut-off photo, always marked "Filled in, not recovered."** Not found in that labelled form in the compared apps.
5. **Offline digital-signature checks against India's CCA roots**, as used on many Aadhaar PDFs. The check stays on the phone. Not found in the compared apps.

Related points that are uncommon in this set, but are described more carefully because a neighbour exists:

- **Edit text inside photos and scans on the device**, with inpainting, without uploading the file. Adobe Scan Premium and Acrobat's paid edit/OCR can change text in scans or PDFs; we could not confirm those paths as a fully on-device, no-upload inpainting editor. Docalto's path is on the phone, with optional online style reading only when you turn Online mode on.
- **Scan and QR through Google's scanners**, so that on most phones this app never opens the camera, with a fallback scanner on de-Googled phones, then a next-step sheet. Other apps scan well; this permission pattern is uncommon.
- **On-device Gemini Nano summaries and answers** when the phone already has the model, with a built-in fallback on every other phone.
- **An encrypted documents backup on shared storage**, offered for restore or deletion after a clear-data or a reinstall, without an account. Other apps restore through their cloud if you have an account. A no-account, on-phone backup that is offered again after uninstall was not found in the compared set; this was not a systematic study of every app's backup folder, so it is not listed in the five points above.

## Notes on each competitor

**Adobe Scan.** A strong, well-known scanner. An Adobe account and Document Cloud sync are part of the normal workflow. Free OCR is capped; several organise, export and text-edit tools are Premium. Files are not described as carrying an Adobe watermark.

**Adobe Acrobat (Android).** A strong PDF reader. Viewing, annotation and Fill & Sign work without paying, and can work offline on a local file. Advanced edit, OCR, convert, compress and organise tools need a subscription, and some of those jobs are online services. Liquid Mode sends the file to Adobe servers and Adobe says it is deleted after the experience is generated. AI Assistant is Adobe's own paid assistant, not a bring-your-own key.

**WPS Office.** A full office suite that also edits PDFs. Useful if you need Writer, Sheets and Slides in the same app. Core local reading and annotation are free; the Play listing contains ads; advanced PDF edit, OCR and conversion may need Pro+. Official pages say local PDFs can be edited without a constant internet connection.

**CamScanner.** A widely used scanner with local and cloud OCR, conversion and sync. The official FAQ states that free exports can carry a CamScanner watermark, and that precise cloud recognition uploads the image.

**Google Drive scan.** Convenient if you already live in Drive. It is a scanner, not a PDF workshop. A Google account is required, and the file is saved to Drive.

**Microsoft Lens and OneDrive.** Lens stopped creating new scans on 9 March 2026 and was removed from the stores earlier that year. Microsoft recommends the OneDrive app. Official documentation says OneDrive does not support saving scans locally. Some Microsoft 365 apps also offer a scan-to-PDF action; those flows still sit inside a Microsoft account.

**Genius Scan.** The closest privacy neighbour among scanner apps in this list: official pages say documents stay on the phone unless you export them, the free Basic plan is unlimited and adds no watermark, and no account is required for that plan. OCR and several extra tools are paid Ultra features. It is a scanner, not a 75-tool PDF editor.

**iLovePDF.** A large, familiar toolkit on the web, desktop and mobile. The website uploads files to servers in the EEA (deleted within two hours). The official mobile rule is mixed: it depends on the tool. Premium adds limits, workflows and extra tools. The desktop app is the official "files never leave this computer" product.

**Xodo.** A well-known Android PDF editor that can read and edit local files offline. Official plan pages describe usage limits and possible watermarks on the free plan. Some conversion, OCR and AI tools are paid, and Xodo's published upload list is for web tools, not a complete Android map.

## Not verified

These were looked for and could not be confirmed from official pages in this pass. They are **not** used as uniqueness claims.

- Whether Adobe Scan's "Edit text in scans" and Acrobat's scanned-page editing run entirely on the device, or with cloud processing.
- The exact mix of on-device and cloud OCR in current Adobe Scan builds (review replies mention cloud OCR; some language packs have been described as on-device).
- Whether WPS Office on Android runs PDF-to-Office and OCR fully on the device, or on a WPS server, when Pro+ is enabled.
- Whether Xodo's Android Office conversion, OCR and PDF translation stay on the device (Xodo's published upload list is for **web** tools).
- Whether PDFelement, Foxit, PDF Extra or another app not in the main table already offers a File Rescue-style tool, a labelled "filled in, not recovered" repair, or offline India CCA signature checks.
- Whether any of the compared apps already writes an encrypted, no-account backup to shared storage that is offered again after uninstall (we did not audit every app's private folders).
- A complete, official count of Indian interface languages for every competitor.
- Exact current prices in every country and store.
- Whether the Microsoft 365 Copilot / Office Android app can save a Scan to PDF only on the device, without OneDrive. Official OneDrive documentation is clear that the OneDrive scanner does not save locally; the Copilot/Office path was not fully mapped in this pass.

## Limits of these claims

Docalto is Android-only. WPS, Acrobat, iLovePDF, Xodo and Microsoft products cover more platforms. WPS and Microsoft 365 are fuller office suites. Acrobat Pro on the desktop remains a deeper professional PDF standard. Genius Scan and Adobe Scan may out-focus Docalto as dedicated cameras. This comparison does not measure OCR accuracy, scan quality, speed or battery use.

## Key points

- Several desktop products go **further** than Docalto as professional PDF or office software. Acrobat Pro, FineReader PDF, Foxit, PDFelement and Nitro officially go deeper on typographic PDF work, OCR, redaction, comparison or batch jobs. WPS Office and Microsoft 365 Word are fuller office suites. That should be said first.
- **Foxit PDF Editor** official 2026.2 pages describe Local AI on the device (after a Microsoft Foundry Local model download) and **Bring Your Own Model** for an OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure. Press material also names GPT and Gemini. So “your own key” is not distinctive against every PC product. Acrobat’s official AI Assistant is Adobe’s own cloud assistant and needs an Adobe ID.
- **PDF24 Creator** is a free Windows toolkit that officially works offline, keeps files on the PC, and needs no account (with named exceptions such as fax, Cloud Print, update checks and OCR language downloads). It is Windows-only, and typographic photo-text inpainting, permanent redaction and visual PDF compare were not found on the official Creator page.
- Desktop OCR editors (especially Acrobat Pro and FineReader) officially make scanned pages searchable and editable, often with deeper typography and layout tools than a phone inpainting editor. Docalto-style inpainting of handwriting in a photo on a PDF page was **not found in those words** on the official PC pages read.
- What remains honest for Docalto: a **phone** toolkit — 75 tools, no account, no document upload, 15 app languages including 11 widely used in India, photo-text inpainting on the device, and Online AI off until you allow it. Among **leading Android apps** (25 November 2026), that combination was not found in the same form. That Android finding still stands. It must not be stretched to mean “not on any PC.”
- Still **not found in the same form** on the official PC pages in this pass (not a worldwide exclusive): File Rescue, a fill marked “Filled in, not recovered,” and offline India CCA signature checks.
