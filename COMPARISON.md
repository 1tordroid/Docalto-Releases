# Why Docalto: how it compares

**PC-class PDF tools in one Android app, running offline on the phone with no account and no upload, which very few apps offer.**

As of November 2026, for Docalto 2.0.1. Facts about other products come from their official pages and may have changed since. Apps change. This is not a paid ranking, and Docalto is not affiliated with any product named here.

## The honest headline

Many merge, split, compress, edit and sign tools already exist. Many PDF websites upload your file to do those jobs, and several desktop programs (PDF24 Creator, Sejda Desktop, Adobe Acrobat Pro, iLovePDF Desktop, a self-hosted Stirling PDF) already run a large toolkit offline on a computer.

What is uncommon is putting that desktop-class set (split by size, pages on one sheet, booklet order, PDF/A, compare, repair, File Rescue, certificate signatures, a multi-step Flow) on an **Android phone**, processed **on the device**, with **no account** and **no document upload**. That is the claim Docalto makes. It is not unique in the world; it is rare as an on-device phone kit.

## Unusual in this set

- **File Rescue and a wide image-format list:** RAW previews, Photoshop, JPEG XL, TIFF, older and professional formats, plus a repair path for damaged pictures. Not advertised by the phone and cloud apps compared here.
- A fill for a cut picture is always marked **"Filled in, not recovered"**.
- **Signature checks against India's CCA roots, offline**, including many Aadhaar PDFs.
- **Scan and QR through Google's scanners**, so on most phones Docalto never opens the camera itself. Phones without Google Play services get an in-app scanner, which asks for the camera when it opens; pictures stay on the phone.
- **Gemini Nano summaries and answers on the phone** when the model is already there, with a built-in fallback on every other phone. Other AI PDF tools in this set run on servers.

## Rare, not unique

- **Flow** (several tools in one run) also exists as iLovePDF Premium workflows and Smallpdf connected workflows, both in the cloud, and as Stirling PDF pipelines on a server you host.
- **Split by size, pages on one sheet, booklet order, PDF/A, compare and repair** are common on desktop (Adobe Acrobat Pro, PDF24 Creator, Sejda Desktop, Stirling PDF). They are uncommon as on-device tools in an Android app that needs no account.
- **Xodo and WPS** can edit local PDFs offline. Acrobat's heavier tools, and most iLovePDF and Smallpdf jobs, need a subscription and often a connection or an upload.

## What Docalto does not do

Docalto does not replace a Windows-only free suite such as PDF24 Creator, or a self-hosted server such as Stirling PDF. It has no iOS or desktop app, and no cloud sync or "request a signature" workflow. Its difference is the on-device, no-account package on a phone.
