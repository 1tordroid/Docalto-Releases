# Docalto 2.6.0

versionName **2.6.0**, versionCode **16**. 25 November 2026 (Asia/Calcutta, IST). Released to internal and closed testing on Google Play.

## Highlights

- **Edit text in photos.** Change words in a scanned page or a photo in your PDF, and keep the original font, size and colour. OCR and inpainting run on the phone.
- **Online AI, off until you allow it.** A switch on Home and in Settings covers AI, translation and cloud tools. When you turn it on, AI can step into tools, flows and search.
- **Your own AI key.** Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, Grok (xAI), or an OpenAI-compatible model. Keys stay encrypted on the phone. No Docalto account.

## Also new

- A cleaner Home, Files, Tools and Settings, in light or dark.
- AI recommends the next step for a recent file, on the phone. Compress can suggest a level with a before and after size.
- Search every tool, file, recent and flow by voice or text.
- A Home widget, app shortcuts, and an encrypted local copy of your work that you can restore after a reinstall.
- 75 tools in All Tools. When you are online, Docalto checks Google Play for updates. If a newer version exists, or no check has succeeded for 45 days, it asks you to update first. Your files are never touched by this.
